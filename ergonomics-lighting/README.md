# Ergonomics & Workspace Lighting Analysis

This directory contains analytical assignments exploring the relationship between environmental factors (specifically lighting) and workspace ergonomics, completed during the Erasmus+ BIP program at the University of Novi Sad.

## Design Assignment: Light and Shade

Evaluating how lighting conditions fundamentally dictate workspace comfort, visual health, and productivity.

### Visual Comparison (`Kyryll1.png` vs. `Kyryll2.png`)
- **Figure 1 (`Kyryll1.png` - Unregulated Lighting):**
  - Dominated by harsh, direct midday sunlight.
  - Strong contrast and severe glare on computer screens.
  - Causes eye strain, reduces readability, and induces fatigue, negatively impacting focus.
- **Figure 2 (`Kyryll2.png` - Optimized Lighting):**
  - Window blinds adjusted to diffuse natural light and eliminate direct glare while preserving ambient brightness.
  - Supplementary warm desk lamp providing balanced task illumination.
  - Results in soft shadows, comfortable contrast, and a calm, focused workspace conducive to sustained productivity.

### Key Takeaway
Lighting is a critical ergonomic parameter that directly influences cognitive well-being and physical comfort. Careful modulation of natural and artificial light sources transforms a fatiguing environment into an optimal, health-supportive workspace.
