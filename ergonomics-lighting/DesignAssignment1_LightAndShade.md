IDEA & COMMENTARY: LIGHTING AND VISUAL COMFORT

My goal for this assignment was to show how lighting fundamentally shapes both the function and the perceived atmosphere of a workspace. The two images compare an unregulated environment to one intentionally adjusted for visual comfort.

In Kyryll1, the scene is dominated by harsh, direct midday sunlight. The high contrast and strong glare on the monitor create a visually aggressive space. This type of uncontrolled lighting causes eye strain, reduces screen readability, and makes the environment feel intense and fatiguing, which can quickly diminish focus and productivity.

In Kyryll2, the lighting has been optimized. The window blinds are closed to diffuse and modulate the natural light, eliminating glare while maintaining ambient brightness. A warm desk lamp is added to provide even, task-specific illumination. This adjustment completely transforms the perception of the room: the harsh shadows soften, visual contrast becomes comfortable, and the space shifts from overwhelming to calm and focused.

This exercise demonstrates that lighting is not just about visibility - it actively dictates mood, comfort, and how we experience a space. By controlling intensity, direction, and glare, we can turn a standard desk area into a health-supportive environment that promotes sustained concentration and well-being.
